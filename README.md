# Team CHAKRA Docs

## What is this?

This is the knowledge base of Team CHAKRA. It is where we record the work we do so that the next team does not start from zero.

Most clubs lose knowledge every time members graduate. Projects get built, break, get fixed, and the reasons are forgotten. 
This repo is our fix: everything we learn gets written down in one place that any member can search.

## What you will find here

- **Tutorials:** explanations of concepts and techniques, such as how stereo vision works. Read these to learn something new before using it in a project.
- **Projects:** a folder for every project we have built, with its design, components, and results.
- **Failure reports:** honest write-ups of what went wrong, why, and what we would do differently. These are the most valuable files in the repo.
- **Success reports:** what worked, why it worked, and what was luck instead of skill.

## Who is it for?

- **New members** who want to see what the club has built and learn the basics of each division and to understand why past decisions were made.
- **Project teams** who want to check whether someone has already solved their problem or made the same mistake.
  
## How it is organised

The repo is split by division (ASD, CRD, IPD). Each division has two folders:
- `tutorials/` for general knowledge
- `projects/` for time-bound work and its reports
